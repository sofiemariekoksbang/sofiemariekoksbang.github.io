<h2>The Welcome to Denmark Library</h2>

<blockquote>
<p>
This page lists the books in my Welcome to Denmark library (situated in my office at SDU). The library is mainly aimed at PhD students and postdocs at CP3. The original idea with the library was to have a few books about living in Denmark but it was immediately extended to also include books on the philosophy of science, leadership and networking. The books are listed on this page so that you (a PhD student or postdoc at CP3) can see them conveniently without having to stand awkwardly in my office, looking to see if there's a book you care to borrow.
</p>
</blockquote>
